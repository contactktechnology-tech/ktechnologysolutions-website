Engagement Models

Five Ways to Work Together.
One Standard of Delivery.

How organisations buy from KTechnology Solutions — from a single assessment to an ongoing technology partnership. No prices and no service-level figures are published; every scope is agreed with you.

Comparison

The Five Engagement Models

A side-by-side view of what each model is, the work it typically covers, and where it usually starts.

ModelTypical workBest suited whenUsual starting point

Managed Support

Recurring support and maintenance for your environment, delivered remotely UK-wide and onsite across the region.

Typical work
  • Helpdesk and user support
  • Monitoring and maintenance
  • Patching and update scheduling
  • Regular service reviews
Best suited when

You want a consistent, documented relationship rather than calling someone when something breaks.

Usual starting point

An onboarding review of your environment.

Project Delivery

Fixed-scope infrastructure, migration, installation and deployment work, delivered to an agreed scope and handed over with documentation.

Typical work
  • Cloud and Microsoft 365 migrations
  • Network deployments
  • Security implementation
  • Device rollouts
  • Business-system implementation
Best suited when

A defined change needs to be delivered to scope and handed over cleanly.

Usual starting point

Discovery and a written scope.

Advisory & Audit

Structured assessment of your environment, concluding with written findings and prioritised recommendations.

Typical work
  • Infrastructure review
  • Cyber security review
  • Network assessment
  • Microsoft 365 review
  • Technology audit
Best suited when

You need an independent, written picture before committing budget or changing provider.

Usual starting point

A consultation and an agreed assessment scope.

Technology Sourcing

Vendor-neutral procurement, deployment and lifecycle management of hardware and licences.

Typical work
  • Hardware sourcing to specification
  • Licensing coordination
  • Asset registration
  • Refresh planning
  • Secure decommissioning
Best suited when

Equipment and licences need to be bought, deployed and tracked consistently across the estate.

Usual starting point

An agreed specification.

Partner & Subcontract Delivery

Engineering capacity for another technology or facilities provider, as principal provider or as the delivery arm behind your organisation.

Typical work
  • Regional field engineering
  • Installation and rollout capacity
  • Project delivery under your processes
  • Completion records to your format
Best suited when

You need dependable regional capacity that follows your method and standards.

Usual starting point

A conversation about scope, method and standards.
