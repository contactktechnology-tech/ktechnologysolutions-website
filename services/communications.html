COM Core discipline

Voice and Collaboration for Distributed Teams

Voice, collaboration tools and meeting-room technology that work reliably wherever your team is working.

The Problem

Communication Failures Are an Operational Cost

Communication infrastructure that does not work reliably is a direct operational cost — in time, productivity, and client experience.

Distributed working requires communication infrastructure that is both dependable and appropriately controlled, across every environment your team works in.
