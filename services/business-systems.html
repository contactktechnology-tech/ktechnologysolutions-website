SYS Core discipline

Operational Systems That Teams Actually Use

Deployment and integration of the operational systems a business runs on — with adoption built in from the start.

The Problem

Implementation Without Adoption

Implementation without adoption is wasted investment. Systems chosen without reference to how the business works end up bypassed, duplicated in spreadsheets or left half-configured.

Technology deployment succeeds when it is matched to how the business actually operates, and when staff are supported through the change.
