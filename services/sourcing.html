Technology Sourcing Across the Whole Lifecycle

Hardware sourcing and licensing coordination, through deployment and asset registration, to refresh planning and secure end-of-life handling.

The Problem

Buying Technology Is the Easy Part

Equipment bought ad hoc arrives unconfigured, goes unrecorded and is retired without a plan for the data it holds. Licences drift out of step with the people who use them.

Treating sourcing as part of a lifecycle — from specification to secure decommissioning — keeps the estate consistent, recorded and accountable.
